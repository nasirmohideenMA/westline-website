About the Project

Cubix — IT Park & Hotel

"Where Mangalore's business community works, meets, and stays — all under one world-class roof."

Cubix is Westline's landmark IT park and hotel complex situated in Kadri — one of Mangalore's most connected and sought-after commercial corridors.

Designed for technology companies, startups, and business travellers, Cubix combines premium Grade-A office spaces with hotel accommodation in a single integrated campus. A fully operational rental asset delivering consistent returns to investors.

The project reflects Westline's vision to diversify beyond residential development — creating commercial landmarks that serve Mangalore's growing professional and business community.
