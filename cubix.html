About The Project

A Business Address
That Means Something

"The proposed tallest commercial tower in South India — and the first address in Mangalore built to match the ambition of the companies inside it."

Mangalore's economy has outgrown its commercial real estate. The city has the talent, the connectivity and the appetite — but businesses looking for genuine Grade-A space have had to compromise on floor plates, power reliability, parking or simply prestige.

Cubix exists to end that compromise. Fifty-three floors on NH66 at Kadri, combining curated retail at the base, large-format office floors through the tower, and a hotel with serviced residences above — so that a company can host a client, run an operation and put up a visiting team without anyone leaving the building.

For an occupier, that means an address that does part of the selling for you. For an investor, it means a mixed-use asset in a market with very little comparable supply.

Why Businesses Will

An office is a recruitment tool, a client impression and an operational asset all at once. Cubix was planned around all three.

Large, efficient floor plates that can be taken whole or subdivided. Expand within the same building instead of relocating every time you hire.

Cafés, restaurants, conference rooms, a banquet hall, a fitness centre and serviced residences. Host a board meeting and accommodate the attendees without leaving the building.
Secure And Professionally Run
