About the Project

Westline Signature

"South India's tallest residential tower — where every sunrise is a panorama and every sunset a spectacle."

Westline Signature stands as South India's tallest residential tower — a 3 Basement + Ground + 50 floor monument to modern luxury on NH66, Nanthoor Hilltop, Mangalore, overlooking the Arabian Sea.

With the first full glass façade residential project in South India, featuring Schuco German windows with Low-E double-glazed units (SHGC 0.335, U-value 1.521), Signature redefines what premium living means in coastal Karnataka. Every apartment is a vantage point — panoramic sea views, verdant Western Ghats, and the glittering city below.

The project spans 36+ world-class amenities across dedicated floors — from an infinity pool and rooftop clubhouse to a business centre, home theatre, and sky lounge. This is not just a home. It is a legacy.
