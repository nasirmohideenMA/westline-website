About The Project

Purpose-Built
For Practitioners

"Healthcare businesses don't need a generic commercial shell — they need power backup that never fails, waiting areas that reassure patients, and neighbours in the same field."

Salubrity was conceived specifically around the needs of clinicians, diagnostic operators and wellness practitioners — a building where a dermatology clinic, a physiotherapy centre and a diagnostic lab can each operate professionally, supported by infrastructure built for exactly that use.

Falnir sits within Mangalore's established medical corridor, close to major hospitals — meaning referral networks, patient familiarity with the area, and a built-in professional ecosystem from day one.
