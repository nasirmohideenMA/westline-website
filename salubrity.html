About the Project

Salubrity

"Wellness Spaces, Prime Location"

Salubrity is Westline's wellness-focused commercial project in Falnir — one of Mangalore's premium residential and healthcare neighbourhoods. Offering polyclinic suites and retail commercial spaces for medical professionals and wellness businesses.

Strategically positioned in Falnir's high-footfall corridor, Salubrity offers excellent visibility, accessibility, and an established patient catchment. Ideal for doctors, clinics, and allied health businesses.
