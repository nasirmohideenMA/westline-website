About The Opportunity

Karnataka's Hill
Country Has A Yield

"Chikmagalur draws visitors every month of the year — coffee tourism, trekking, weekend escapes from Bengaluru and Mangalore. Ohana turns that traffic into an income stream for its owners."

Ohana Luxury Stays sits at Mullayanagiri — Karnataka's highest peak and one of its most visited hill destinations. Rather than selling holiday homes that sit empty most of the year, Ohana is structured as a professionally managed resort: you own the unit, we handle the guests, the bookings, the housekeeping and the marketing.

This is built for buyers who see the appeal of hill-country real estate but don't want the operational headache of running a homestay themselves.
