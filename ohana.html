About the Project

Ohana Luxury Stays

"Where the Clouds Meet Your Doorstep"

Ohana Luxury Stays is Westline's boutique resort nestled in the misty highlands of Mullayanagiri — Karnataka's highest peak — at approximately 1,900 metres above sea level in the heart of Chikmagalur's coffee country.

Each cottage is designed to frame the mountains, the clouds, and the vast coffee estates below. From bonfire evenings and trekking trails to in-cottage dining and coffee plantation tours — Ohana is a truly extraordinary escape.
