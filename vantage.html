About The Project

Own The Corner
Everyone Notices

"A corner plot on two live roads, in a locality that never stops moving — this is commercial real estate that markets itself."

Kankanady is one of Mangalore's busiest commercial and civic junctions — home to the district court, government offices, established businesses and a constant flow of footfall. Vantage occupies the corner of Fr. Muller Road and Balikashram Road, giving every unit dual-road visibility that interior properties simply cannot offer.

Ten floors of premium commercial space, designed for retail at street level and offices through the tower — built to the same specification standard as Signature, at a fraction of the ticket size.
