How It Works

Four Steps. No Ambiguity.

From registration to payout, you always know exactly where you stand.

01
Register

Fill the form below with your RERA agent details. We verify and activate your partner account, usually within two working days.

02
Register Your Client

Before the site visit, submit your client's name and number. That lead is now formally attributed to you for the full protection period.

03
We Close Together

Our sales team handles the site visit, documentation and negotiation. You stay in the loop at every stage — and stay the owner of the relationship.

04
Get Paid

Commission is released on the agreed trigger event, within the published payout window. Statement issued with every payment.
