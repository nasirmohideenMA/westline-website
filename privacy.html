1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data that you choose to provide directly to us, primarily through the enquiry, callback, and registration forms on this Site. Depending on which form you use, this may include:

If you are...We may collect
A prospective buyer or investorName, mobile number, email, city, project of interest, unit type, and your purchase timeline
A channel partner (agent)Name, agency name, mobile number, email, city of operation, K-RERA agent registration number, and years of experience
A landownerName, mobile number, email, the general location and approximate size of your land, and ownership structure
Anyone submitting a general enquiryName, mobile number, email, and the content of your message

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect sensitive personal data (such as financial account details, health information, or government ID numbers) through this Site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry, callback request, or site-visit request;
  • To assess and process channel partner or landowner registrations;
  • To route your enquiry to the correct internal team (sales, leasing, partnerships, or construction services) and record it in our internal customer relationship management system;
  • To send you project updates or offers, but only where you have specifically opted in to receive them;
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and related K-RERA regulations, where you have booked or expressed formal interest in a unit.

We do not use your personal data for any purpose beyond what is described in this Policy without seeking your fresh consent.

3. Consent

Where our forms include a consent checkbox — such as agreeing to be contacted, or opting in to marketing updates — we process your data only on the basis of that consent. You may withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 15; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out, or our ability to respond to an active enquiry you have raised with us.

4. How We Share Your Information

We keep the circle of people who see your information as small as the task requires. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Internal teams: the relevant sales, leasing, or partnerships team handling your specific enquiry, and our internal CRM/ERP system used to track it;
  • Banking partners: only where you have explicitly requested home loan assistance, and only to the specific bank or NBFC you have asked us to connect you with;
  • Service providers: providers who send email or SMS/WhatsApp notifications on our behalf, bound by confidentiality obligations, and used solely to deliver the communication you initiated;
  • Legal or regulatory authorities: where disclosure is required by law, court order, or a valid request from K-RERA or another competent authority.

We do not share your data with unrelated third-party marketing platforms, data brokers, or lead-resale networks.

5. Landowner & Confidential Enquiries

Enquiries submitted through our Landowners page are treated with additional care. These are reviewed only by a small senior partnership team and are never shared with our general sales staff, channel partners, or marketing systems. If you have asked to be contacted in a specific way (for example, only by phone, or only outside your usual working hours), we will make reasonable efforts to honour that preference.

6. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it has been processed;
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • Erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to any legal requirement that we retain it;
  • Withdraw consent for any processing that is based on your consent, at any time;
  • Grievance redressal — to raise a complaint about how your data has been handled, and to have it addressed within a reasonable time;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer using the details in Section 13. We may need to verify your identity before actioning certain requests.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy. As a general guide: enquiry and callback data not converted into a booking is typically retained for a limited period to allow follow-up, after which it may be deleted on request or as part of routine data hygiene; data connected to a booked unit is retained for the duration required under RERA record-keeping obligations and applicable limitation periods. You may request earlier deletion at any time, subject to these legal requirements.

8. Cookies

This Site may use cookies or similar technologies to remember basic preferences and understand how the Site is used, in order to improve it. Where cookies are in use, you will be informed through an on-site notice and given the option to manage your preference. You can also control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though this may affect some functionality of the Site.

9. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the data we hold, including restricting internal access to enquiry data to relevant staff, using secure connections for data transmitted through our forms, and periodically reviewing who has access to our CRM/ERP systems. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to affect you, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals in accordance with the timelines and requirements prescribed under the DPDP Act, including a description of the nature of the breach and the steps being taken in response.

11. Children's Data

This Site is intended for use by adults capable of entering into a property transaction and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will take steps to delete it.

12. Third-Party Links

This Site may contain links to third-party platforms, such as Google Maps, YouTube, or Instagram. These platforms operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the data practices of any third-party site linked from ours.
