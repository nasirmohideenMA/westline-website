Our Story

Building Mangalore's
Future

Westline Builders Private Limited has been reshaping Mangalore's skyline since 2014. Founded on the principles of quality, transparency, and customer-first delivery, we have grown from a respected local developer to one of the region's most ambitious real estate houses.

From South India's tallest residential tower on NH66 to polyclinic hubs, IT parks, luxury resorts, and serviced apartments — every Westline project carries a singular promise: what we build will endure in structure, in value, and in the lives of those who call it home.

Quality First
