Our Story

Building Mangalore's
Future

Westline Builders Private Limited has shaped the Mangalore skyline for over a decade and a half. Founded on the principles of quality, transparency, and customer-first delivery, we have grown from a respected local developer to one of the region's most ambitious real estate houses.

From our flagship 50-floor Signature tower on NH66 to our commercial ventures in Kankanady, every Westline project carries a singular promise: that what we build will endure — in structure, in value, and in the lives of those who call it home.
